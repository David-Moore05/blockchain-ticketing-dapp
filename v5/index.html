ℹ️ HOW IT WORKS

  • Create or load an Ethereum wallet — no MetaMask required
  • Get free Sepolia ETH from a faucet to pay for tickets and gas fees
  • Buy tickets: send 0.00001 SETH per ticket to the smart contract
  • At the door, transfer your ticket token to the vendor to gain entry

📖 WHAT IS A KEYSTORE?

  • Address: Your public identifier — share this freely so others can send you funds
  • Private Key: Signs transactions on your behalf — NEVER share this with anyone, ever
  • Keystore JSON: Your private key encrypted with your password using AES-128-CTR + scrypt key derivation
  • Store the keystore file and your password in a safe place — neither can be recovered if lost

🚪 WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU SEND?

  • Your TCKT token moves from your wallet to the doorman's wallet permanently
  • The blockchain records this transfer forever — it cannot be reversed
  • The doorman's TCKT balance increases — the venue can track total entries
  • You need a tiny amount of SETH (not tickets) to pay the network gas fee
